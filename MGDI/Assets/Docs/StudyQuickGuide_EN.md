# Study Quick Guide

## 1. Overview

- This study includes 4 conditions and 3 tasks.
- Conditions: `Macro - Near Head`, `Macro - Side Of Body`, `Micro - Near Head`, `Micro - Side Of Body`
- Tasks: `Placement`, `Rotation`, `Scaling`
- The condition order may vary depending on the `Participant ID`.
- In both `Macro` and `Micro`, submission is always `triple tap` for every task.

## 2. Procedure

1. On the start screen, enter your assigned `Participant ID`.
2. If needed, use `Edit` to change it, then press `Continue`.
3. In the runtime scene, when you see `Scan the QR code to begin.`, scan the QR code.
4. When a condition starts, follow the posture instruction and example image, then hold that posture briefly.
5. After the countdown, the task starts automatically. There is no separate `Start` button.
6. A short `practice` may appear before each task. `Practice` is not logged.
7. Match the target, then `triple tap` when you are ready to submit.
8. After one condition ends, the next condition starts automatically.

## 3. Condition Summary

### Macro - Near Head

- Use larger arm and hand movements.
- Keep your hand near your head.

![Macro Near Head](../Image/MacroNearHead.png)

### Macro - Side Of Body

- Use larger arm and hand movements.
- Keep your hand at the side of your body.
- In this condition, the input is remapped.
- Up/down hand movement is remapped to forward/back movement, and forward/back movement is remapped to up/down movement.

![Macro Side Of Body](../Image/MacroSideBody.png)

### Micro - Near Head

- Keep your hand near your head.
- Use phone screen swipes for fine control.

![Micro Near Head](../Image/MacroNearHead.png)

### Micro - Side Of Body

- Keep your hand at the side of your body.
- Use phone screen swipes for fine control.

![Micro Side Of Body](../Image/MicroSideBody.png)

## 4. Task Summary

### Placement

- Move the tool to the target position.
- In `Micro`, swipe on the phone to move the tool left/right and up/down.
- If needed, `double tap` to switch the movement plane for forward/back adjustment.
- When ready, submit with `triple tap`.

### Rotation

- Match the target orientation.
- In `Micro`, left/right swipes control yaw, and up/down swipes control roll.
- If needed, `double tap` to switch the vertical swipe axis between `roll` and `pitch`.
- When ready, submit with `triple tap`.

### Scaling

- Match the target size.
- In `Micro`, up/down swipes make the tool larger or smaller.
- When ready, submit with `triple tap`.

## 5. Notes

- In `Macro`, you control the task mainly with arm and hand movement.
- In `Micro`, you keep the instructed hand posture and use phone swipes for fine adjustments.
- If the proxy hand is close enough to an object, it can grab the object.
- In `Macro`, the object is held while you keep touching the screen, and it is released when you let go.
- In `Micro`, a single tap grabs the object, and another tap releases it.
- Try not to change posture too much while the instruction image and countdown are shown.
- If QR scanning fails or the display/input seems wrong, tell the facilitator immediately.

## 6. Most Important

- Before starting, enter your `Participant ID` and scan the `QR` code.
- Keep your hand in the correct condition posture: `Near Head` or `Side Of Body`.
- In `Macro`, you hold an object while touching the screen. In `Micro`, you tap to grab and tap again to release.
- In `Micro`, use phone swipes to control the task.
- In `Macro - Side Of Body`, up/down and forward/back movement are remapped.
- Submission is always `triple tap` for every task.
